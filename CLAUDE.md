## Pull Request Review Workflow

- Collect all review comments before making changes.
- Validate each comment; do not implement incorrect or purely stylistic suggestions.
- Fix accepted comments as one batch, run tests, and push once.
- Do not push separately after every individual fix.
- Perform at most two review/fix rounds.
- After two rounds, summarize remaining disagreements and wait for human direction.
